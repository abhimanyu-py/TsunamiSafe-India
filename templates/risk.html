Tsunami Risk in India

Learn about tsunami hazards and coastal exposure.

What is a tsunami?

A tsunami is a series of ocean waves usually caused by underwater earthquakes, volcanic activity or landslides.

Why can India be affected?

India has a long coastline along the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal and Indian Ocean. Some coastal areas may be exposed to tsunami hazards.

Areas to understand

  • Andaman and Nicobar Islands
  • Eastern coast along the Bay of Bengal
  • Western coast along the Arabian Sea

Actual risk depends on the source event, coastal geography and official hazard assessments.
