Emergency Safety Guide

Prioritise safety and follow official instructions.

Recognise natural warning signs

  • A strong or long earthquake near the coast.
  • An unusual rapid rise or retreat of seawater.
  • A loud roaring sound from the sea.

What should you do?

  1. Move away from the coast immediately.
  2. Reach higher ground or an officially designated safe location.
  3. Help others only when it is safe to do so.
  4. Do not return until authorities say it is safe.

Do not go to the beach to watch a tsunami or wait for a visual confirmation.
