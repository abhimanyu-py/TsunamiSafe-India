Tsunami Preparedness

Simple actions can help you prepare before a disaster.

Before a tsunami

  • Learn local evacuation routes and safe areas.
  • Keep an emergency kit with water, food, a torch and essential supplies.
  • Save important emergency contacts.
  • Plan how family members will communicate.

During a warning

  • Follow official alerts and evacuation instructions immediately.
  • Move inland or to higher ground as instructed.
  • If you feel a strong or long earthquake near the coast, move to safety without waiting for an alert.
  • Stay away from the shore until officials declare it safe.
